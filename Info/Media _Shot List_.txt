Essential Photos Needed Per Branch:
The Exterior: A clear daytime shot of the front of the building and entrance.


Room Variations: At least one clear photo of each package type (Single room, 2-seat room, 6-seat AC room, etc.). Show the beds, desks, and storage.


Washrooms: 1-2 photos of clean bathrooms (this is a top priority for students).


Common Areas: The dining room/canteen area, study halls, or lounges.


Security Features: A photo of the security desk, fingerprint scanner (if any), or CCTV cameras to build trust.


The Extra Perks: For the girls' branch, specifically photograph the exclusive perks (e.g., in-house gym, secure gate).


Recommended Videos (1 per branch):
The Walk-Through: You don't need fancy equipment. Take your phone, start at the front gate, walk into the lobby, show the dining area, and walk into one of your best rooms. A 45-to-60-second tour builds massive trust.
