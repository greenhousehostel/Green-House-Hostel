Hostel Policies

Universal Rules

Curfew Timings

Residents must return by 11:00 PM (in girls hostel at 7:00 PM, approval from guardian needed to stay out longer). Late entries require prior permission from management.

Payment Deadlines

Rent must be paid by the 7th of every month. Late payments may incur a penalty.

Visitor Policies

Visitors are allowed in the common lobby. Parents are allowed to stay over with prior management permission.

Security Deposit

A security deposit is required at check-in, refundable upon leaving with a 1-month notice.
